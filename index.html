Camera & Recognition

Camera starts after permission. If the preferred camera fails, RITA will try a generic camera fallback.
Lower values accept a more distant clinical framing. Increase only for close nasal detail.

The app uses visual commands plus beep-only alignment feedback. Capture remains locked until face size, angle, yaw, stability, and nasal-tip alignment are correct. A camera shutter sound plays after each capture when enabled. No spoken voice commands are used.
